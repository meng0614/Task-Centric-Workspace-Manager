# Skill Management

Skills are categorized by purpose:

- `common`: infrastructure and general utilities.
- `media`: publishing, images, video, social media, article workflows.
- `research`: papers, experiments, literature, presentations, scientific work.
- `experimental`: unverified, risky, temporary, reverse-engineered, or ambiguous Skills.
- `archive`: deprecated or replaced Skills.

Skill directories should use:

```text
SkillName__ShortDescription
```

The Skill registry is generated at:

```text
E:\codex\skills\skill_registry.md
```
